FOR THE CURIOUS

Under the hood

What was counted?

Adjacent A–Z letters within words, using the all-word-lengths and all-positions bigram counts described by Peter Norvig. Word endings contribute no outgoing transition. These are corpus frequencies, not a rule for every writer or every context.

The notebook uses 669 observed pairs with 2,819,662,855,499 total occurrences, from the CSV copy at Git blob bac34b65ea3c8380939e25d6195a12667c0ce524. Missing pairs have zero observed count; no smoothing is applied.

Probabilities, paths, and the picture

Probability of the next letter = pair count ÷ all outgoing counts for the current letter. Strongest links use the largest count, with alphabetical tie-breaking. This is deterministic greedy choice, not random sampling from the distribution.

Animation follows the exported frames exactly. Paths are counted separately after merging; the loop count includes both E and R. Node positions are fixed for readability and carry no statistical meaning. The two directions of E ↔ R use separate curves.

In a letter preview, every observed outgoing pair is drawn, including self-loops. Stroke width grows linearly with probability, with a thin visibility baseline so rare pairs remain visible. Zero-count edges are omitted from the graph but retained in the table. Percentages are rounded only for display.
