THE INTERESTING BIT

More games.
Not always a better champion.

In this batch, a seeded knockout cuts the average gap from 132.0 to 106.9 points at the same 31-match cost as a random draw.

The 992-match league has the smallest average gap among the tested settings: 29.1 points. But adding best-of-seven playoffs increases it to 43.3. Extra games can give a lower-rated team another route to the title.

These are results for these rules and this model—not a universal ranking of tournament formats. There’s no single “best” without deciding how many matches you can afford and what gap is acceptable.

FOR THE CURIOUS

Under the hood

The match model & what “best” means

“Best” means highest input FIFA rating, not independently verified true ability. All 32 men’s national teams retain their snapshot ratings throughout every tournament.

P(A wins) = 1 / (1 + 10 ** (−(rating_A − rating_B) / 600))

Each match is an independent win/loss draw, conditional on ratings. No draws, home advantage, fatigue or rating updates. We borrow FIFA’s expected-result curve as a simplified W/L model; this is not an official or calibrated win forecast.

Exact tournament rules
  • Random knockout: random permutation into a fixed 32-team bracket; 31 matches.
  • Seeded knockout: recursive full seeding, 1 vs 32, 2 vs 31, etc.; seeds 1 and 2 can meet only in the final; 31 matches.
  • World Cup: four pots of eight by FIFA rank, one random team from each pot per group. Eight groups play a single round robin. Top two advance. First knockout pairings: A1–B2, C1–D2, E1–F2, G1–H2 | B1–A2, D1–C2, F1–E2, H1–G2. No geographic restrictions or third-place match; 63 matches.
  • League: double round robin; most wins wins the title; 992 matches.
  • League + best-of-seven: the same saved league season, then top 16 seeded by standings into a fixed bracket. First to four wins per series; no reseeding. Theoretical total: 1,052–1,097 matches, regardless of the observed range.
  • Swiss: 5, 7 or 9 rounds, 16 matches each. Full pairings minimise total absolute current-win differences; small random weights break pairing ties. No repeat opponents, byes or backtracking. Infeasible pairing raises an error. This is an educational variant.

Every standings tie—including group advancement and the league or Swiss title—is resolved by random lots, never rating. The browser demo implements only the two knockout formats. Browser randomness does not reproduce the Python batch seed.

Saved batch & estimation uncertainty

1,000 tournaments per setting, Python random seed 42. League and league-plus-playoffs share the same season within each simulation; their outcomes are paired. Other formats use separate random streams.

Monte Carlo standard error = sample standard deviation of rating gaps / √1,000. The approximate 95% interval is mean ± 1.96 × SE. It estimates the model-conditional long-run average, not the spread of individual champions or confidence in our assumptions.

No untested formats are interpolated, and no universal optimum is claimed.
